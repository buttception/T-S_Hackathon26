Adaptive CGF Trainer: how the program runs

A training session is a short exchange of messages between three pages, a simulator that steps through time, and a slower loop that adjusts the computer-generated forces from what the trainee does. When the run ends, the result flows back into stored profiles. This map follows cgf-demo/ in the repo as of 470774f.

1. One session, message by message

Time runs downward. Each arrow is a call or a postMessage; the simulator talks only to the host page. The orange dashed box is where the run happens, drawn in the next section. Everything is saved in the browser, so it all works from file://.

2. Inside the simulator: two loops

The fast loop is the simulation. The slow loop watches its events and changes how the CGFs score the trainee's behaviour. The run ends when simulated time reaches the duration (2400 s by default) in both adaptive and fixed mode, so every run gathers the same amount of evidence.

Starts fromThe config from init: scenario, crew, seed, mode (adaptive or fixed), the instructor's envelope (min, base and max for each setting), duration and cycle length. Each setting begins at its base.

Fast loop: the simulation

Every frame in the browser, in steps of at most 2 s of simulated time. Same seed and inputs give the same events.

  1. 1
    Advance time

    Every frame, CGF.tick turns real time into simulated time (60 times faster by default) and cuts it into steps of at most 2 s.

  2. 2
    Move the own ship

    Orders come from the trainee at the keyboard, or from the scripted crew agent in a linked run. Malfunctions act here.

  3. 3
    Score each CGF unit

    Factors such as proximity, closest point of approach, territorial waters and ignored warnings, each times its weight multiplier, add up to a tension that decays toward a baseline.

  4. 4
    Pick a state and act

    Tension selects the unit's state (Patrol, Shadow, Warn, Harass, Weapons ready), after a reaction delay. The unit steers, hails or warns.

  5. 5
    Share with the force

    Units of one force share the last known fix and alerts, and escalate together. How much depends on the force's teamwork setting.

  6. 6
    Record what happened

    Hails acknowledged or ignored, safe passages and close quarters become events, each tagged with the seat that caused it.

↓events from step 6 feed the slow loop

Slow loop: adaptation

Every 30 s of simulated time.

  1. 1
    Collect events

    The events logged since the last cycle. The cycle runs every 30 s of simulated time.

  2. 2
    Pick the target

    Per seat (AI partners are skipped): the weakest focus competency with enough evidence in the working profile. The working profile starts from history and is updated by this run's events.

  3. 3
    Read the live signal

    Success rate over the last 10 target events, against a band of 40 to 70 per cent. Above it: raise the pressure. Below it: ease off. Too few events: raise exposure.

  4. 4
    Choose settings

    The settings that exercise that competency, for example the weight on ignored warnings. Each change is tagged live, history or team.

  5. 5
    Clamp to the envelope

    No value leaves the instructor's min and max. In fixed assessment mode the cycle returns no adjustments at all.

  6. 6
    Apply

    CGF.apply sets parameters and weight multipliers. It never moves a unit or changes the score or outcome. The reasoning panel lists the change, its evidence and its cause.

↑new settings change how step 3 scores units from the next step on
When time is upThe run ends with outcome success, or fail if a failure state was reached. The engine builds the session record (events, adjustments, outcome, seed, config) and sends ended and record to the host. The instructor can also act at any time: injects and typed steering go through a validated directive, never straight to the engine.
